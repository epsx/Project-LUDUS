High-level interpretation — 06.09.2026

  The project is beyond the platform-proof stage.

  The PC executable has a stable native window/render startup path,
  retail filesystem/config startup is running, global texture metadata
  is loaded, core runtime managers are being brought up in original
  order, and MusyX now builds deeply enough on MSVC x64 that the
  remaining warnings expose real 32-bit/Dolphin assumptions rather
  than basic import/build failures.

  converting/uploading the real GameCube texture/render payloads into
  the native WebGPU path and continuing the retail startup chain far
  enough to produce the first genuine rendered game frame.
